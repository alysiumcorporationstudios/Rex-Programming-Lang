# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want Rex to be a place where anyone can contribute a good idea without being
made to feel small. We pledge to make participation welcoming regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds this community:

- Being kind and specific in reviews. Say what would make the change better.
- Assuming good faith, and asking a question before assuming a mistake.
- Giving credit where it is due.
- Accepting that maintainers say no to keep the language small, and that a "no"
  is about the idea, not about you.

Examples of behaviour that does not belong here:

- Insults, personal or political attacks, and dismissive comments.
- Sexualised language or attention of any kind.
- Harassment in public or private, including unwelcome contact after a request
  to stop.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, and anywhere you are representing Rex.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at
**alysium.corporation.studios@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer
only. We will respond as quickly as we can and will keep the reporter's identity
confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that break this code, and
may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behaviour they judge
harmful. Decisions will be explained to the person affected, with an invitation
to respond.
